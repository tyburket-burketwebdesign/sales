🏷️ Listing Calls
Only businesses already paying for shared leads on HomeAdvisor, Angi, Thumbtack or similar. They already spend money to get customers, they already hate shared leads, and the opener is built around that pain. The hub scans each business's Google listing and website for a lead-platform badge or profile, and only keeps the ones that show one.
How it works: build the list, then click Thumbtack, Angi or HomeAdvisor on a row. It opens a Google search for that exact business on that site. If their profile shows up, hit ✓ next to it. Checked businesses jump to the top and Start Calling dials those first. Ones with a badge on their own website are already tagged.

👥 Referral partners
⚙️ Program setup
The deal: a business they send signs a monthly plan and pays the first invoice, they get a credit equal to that first month. Website projects earn 10%, up to $500. No cap. Credits have no cash value, expire after 12 months, and need the account in good standing.
Order of operations: 1. Add the partner. 2. Send agreement (DocuSeal). 3. Send their link. 4. Work the referral. 5. When their first invoice is paid in Wave, click First invoice paid. 6. Apply the credit on the partner's next Wave invoice and click Applied.
